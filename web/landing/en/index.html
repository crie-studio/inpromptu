00:22 How it works

It reads along with you, word by word.

It doesn't just scroll when it hears sound. InPromptu knows which word you are saying and where it is in your script.

  1. Listens

    A voice detector (Silero) tells speech from noise every 32 milliseconds. When you go quiet, the text stops.

  2. Recognizes

    The recognizer (Vosk) only looks for the words in your script. It gets hard names right and responds in about half a second.

  3. Aligns

    Each phrase it hears is matched against the whole text. Swapped, skipped or extra words don't lose your place.

  4. Follows

    Your position moves at your pace, which the app learns and remembers for next time.

The real alignment, with the app's own scoring: on top, what the recognizer heard; on the left, the script. The yellow path is where you are.

Match"moves" was heard as "move". They are close enough to count as the same word.
Skipped"and" was not said. The script moves on without waiting for it.
Extra"uh" was said but is not in the script. It is ignored and your position stays put.

      03:45 FAQ

      Questions about the teleprompter.

      Is InPromptu free?

      Yes. It is free and open source (MIT license), with no account, no ads and no paid version.

      How does the teleprompter follow my voice?

      It recognizes the words you say, on your own computer, and matches them against your script. The text moves at your pace, stops when you stop and finds you again if you skip a sentence.

      Does it work offline?

      Yes. After you download the voice model once (31 MB for Portuguese, 40 MB for English), recognition runs offline. No audio ever leaves your computer.

      Does it work with OBS Studio?

      Yes. InPromptu starts and stops the recording with the reading, switches scenes when the script has [scene: Name] and adds chapters. In the Windows app, the teleprompter can also be hidden from recordings.

      Is there a version for Mac, Linux, Chromebook or iPad?

      The web version runs in the browser on any system, best in Chrome and Edge. The installable app is for Windows 10 and 11.

      Can I control it from my phone?

      Yes. Scan a QR code and control the reading from your phone browser, with nothing to install: pause, go back a line, jump to a section and change the speed.

      Which languages does voice recognition understand?

      Portuguese, English and Spanish in the web version. The Windows app also understands French, German and Italian. The classic and voice-activated modes work with any language.

      Is the interface available in English?

      Yes. The interface is in English and Portuguese and follows your system or browser language. You can change it under More → Language.

      Does it work with a beam-splitter teleprompter?

      Yes. Full screen can be mirrored horizontally, vertically or both, and a mirrored second screen can go to the glass monitor.

      Does it make subtitles?

      Yes. When reading by voice, every word is logged at the moment it was said, and the .srt file is ready when you finish.
